Where You Can Find ARCADELX

ARCADELX is designed to operate in a wide range of public and private venue types. Below are venue categories where the kiosk is suited — venues listed are categories only and are not actual locations.

  • Malls
  • Restaurants
  • Hotels
  • Schools
  • Corporate offices
  • Gyms
  • Game zones
  • Entertainment centres
  • Events and exhibitions

Venue considerations

  • Suitable operating space and a clear play area are required. Recommended minimum free area varies by game; consult game details in the catalogue.
  • Electricity and internet connectivity may be required for payment processing and remote updates.
  • Venue staff should provide general supervision; ARCADELX is designed to be easy for venue staff to manage but regular checks and upkeep are recommended.

If you represent a venue and want ARCADELX at your location, visit Become a Partner or contact info@nileegames.com.

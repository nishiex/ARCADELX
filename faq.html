Frequently Asked Questions

What is ARCADELX?

ARCADELX is a motion-sensing gaming kiosk platform by Nilee Games and Future Technologies Pvt. Ltd. It enables short, controller-free gaming sessions using sensors that interpret player movements.

How does motion sensing work?

The kiosk uses depth and motion sensors to detect player position and gestures. Sensors provide motion vectors that the game maps to in-game actions. The system processes motion data in real time for responsive gameplay.

Do I need a controller?

No. ARCADELX games are designed for controller-free play using body movement and gestures.

Does ARCADELX store photos or video?

ARCADELX does not intentionally capture or store player photographs or video recordings. Motion-sensing data may be processed temporarily for gameplay. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I pay?

Scan the QR code displayed on the kiosk or use the on-kiosk payment options if available. Payment is completed through secure payment providers. Contact venue staff or info@nileegames.com if you have payment issues.

What happens if payment succeeds but the game does not start?

Follow on-screen troubleshooting, contact venue staff, and if needed email info@nileegames.com with your transaction reference so we can investigate.

Can I cancel before payment?

Yes. You can change or cancel a selection before payment without fee. No charge occurs until payment is completed.

Are completed sessions refundable?

Completed/activated sessions are generally non-refundable. Refunds may be considered for verified technical, payment, or operational failures — see Refund & Cancellation Policy.

What should I do if a payment is pending?

If a payment is pending, wait a short period for confirmation, check with venue staff, and contact info@nileegames.com with the transaction reference if the status does not resolve.

Is ARCADELX suitable for children?

Many games include age and safety guidance. Children and minors should be supervised by an adult according to venue rules. Check specific game details for recommended ages and accessibility modes.

What should I do if there is a technical problem?

Contact venue staff first. If the issue requires escalation, email info@nileegames.com with details, session ID, and any error messages.

How can I contact support?

Email info@nileegames.com for general support, partnerships, or privacy inquiries.

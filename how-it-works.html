How ARCADELX Works — Step by step

A simple, clear flow so first-time users know exactly what to expect.

  1. 1. Browse available games

    At the kiosk screen (or on the companion site), browse the catalogue and preview session length and price.

  2. 2. Select a game

    Tap or touch to select — game details show expected session time, required space, and a brief gameplay summary.

  3. 3. Review session details and price

    Before payment you will see the exact session price and any optional add-ons. You may change the selection without penalty prior to payment.

  4. 4. Scan the QR code

    The kiosk displays a QR code. Scan it with your phone to proceed to a secure payment page or use the on-kiosk payment options where available.

  5. 5. Complete payment

    Complete the payment through the selected provider. The kiosk shows a clear progress indicator while the payment is processed.

  6. 6. Payment confirmation

    When the transaction is confirmed, you receive an on-screen confirmation and (if you provided contact details) an emailed receipt.

  7. 7. Start the gaming session

    Follow on-screen instructions and the kiosk sensor will interpret your motion to control gameplay. Staff are available at most partnered venues for assistance.

Important notes for first-time users

  • You may browse and change games before payment without a cancellation fee.
  • Successfully activated gaming sessions are generally non-refundable — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
  • If the payment succeeds but the game does not start, follow the on-screen troubleshooting steps and contact venue staff or info@nileegames.com.

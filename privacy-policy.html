Privacy & Data Handling — ARCADELX

We prioritize transparency. This page explains what sensors collect, how data is used, and what ARCADELX does not do.

Motion sensing and gameplay

ARCADELX uses depth and motion sensors to interpret player movement for real-time gameplay. Motion-sensing data may be processed temporarily for session continuity and gameplay responsiveness.

No intentional photography or facial recognition

  • ARCADELX does not intentionally capture or store player photographs or video recordings.
  • The system is not intended to identify individual players and does not intentionally create facial recognition profiles.

Payment data and sensitive credentials

Sensitive payment credentials such as UPI PINs, CVV numbers, and banking passwords are not collected or stored by ARCADELX. Payment flows are handled by secure payment providers; consult the payment provider’s terms for their handling of credentials.

Types of data processed

Examples of information that may be processed for service delivery:

  • Session metadata: game selected, session start/end time, duration, and session identifiers.
  • Transaction information: payment method (type), transaction reference, amount and billing receipt for the session.
  • Motion-sensing input data: raw or processed motion vectors used to drive gameplay; this may be processed only for the duration necessary for gameplay and not long-term stored except for troubleshooting or analytics where explicitly noted.

Support, troubleshooting and analytics

To help resolve operational issues, limited logs or session metadata may be retained. Any retained data will be handled under applicable privacy laws and our full Privacy Policy. For questions, contact info@nileegames.com.

For the full legal text and data subject rights, review the full Privacy Policy (legal) or contact info@nileegames.com.

ARCADELX PRIVACY POLICY

Nilee Games and Future Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Nilee Games", "Company", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information processed through ARCADELX, our motion-sensing gaming kiosk and associated digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

This policy is designed with reference to India's applicable data-protection framework, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

1. ABOUT ARCADELX

ARCADELX is a motion-sensing gaming and entertainment platform developed and operated by Nilee Games and Future Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Users may interact with ARCADELX through a physical gaming kiosk, QR-based payment system, games, and associated digital services.

2. IMPORTANT PRIVACY STATEMENT

ARCADELX DOES NOT CAPTURE OR STORE USER IMAGES OR PHOTOGRAPHS.

The camera/sensor technology used by ARCADELX is intended for real-time motion sensing and gameplay interaction.

ARCADELX does not intentionally capture, record, save, or store photographs or video recordings of players for its gaming operation.

The motion-sensing system may process movement information temporarily during gameplay to enable the game to respond to the player's movements.

We do not use the ARCADELX camera as a photography, CCTV or video-recording system.

3. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on how you use ARCADELX, we may process the following categories of information.

A. Gaming / Session Data

This may include:

  • Kiosk ID;
  • Game selected;
  • Game/session ID;
  • Session start and end time;
  • Session duration;
  • Game score;
  • Game performance/statistics;
  • Game completion information;
  • Number of sessions;
  • Gameplay-related events;
  • Technical/gameplay status.

This information is primarily used to operate, maintain and improve ARCADELX.

B. Transaction Data

When you pay for an ARCADELX gaming session, we may receive or maintain transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction ID;
  • Payment status;
  • Payment amount;
  • Date and time of transaction;
  • Kiosk/location ID;
  • Order/session reference;
  • Payment method or payment-provider reference;
  • Refund status, where applicable.

We do not store your complete debit-card, credit-card, UPI PIN, CVV or banking credentials.

Payments may be processed by third-party payment service providers, and their respective privacy policies may also apply.

4. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT THROUGH ARCADELX

ARCADELX is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Unless specifically stated otherwise for a particular service, ARCADELX does not intentionally collect or store:

  • Player photographs;
  • Player video recordings;
  • Facial-recognition profiles;
  • Face biometric templates;
  • Fingerprints;
  • Voice recordings;
  • Aadhaar number;
  • PAN number;
  • Bank account passwords;
  • UPI PIN;
  • Credit/debit card CVV;
  • Other sensitive authentication credentials.

The camera/sensing hardware is used for the intended motion-sensing gameplay functionality and not for storing player images.

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information processed through ARCADELX may be used for the following purposes:

Gameplay

To:

  • Detect player movement;
  • Enable motion-based gameplay;
  • Start and manage gaming sessions;
  • Record scores and game results;
  • Maintain game progress where applicable.

Payment Processing

To:

  • Confirm payments;
  • Activate paid gaming sessions;
  • Verify failed or pending transactions;
  • Process eligible refunds;
  • Resolve payment-related complaints.

Operations

To:

  • Monitor kiosk status;
  • Detect technical problems;
  • Maintain the ARCADELX platform;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions;
  • Maintain transaction and operational records.

Analytics and Improvement

We may use aggregated or appropriately de-identified information to understand:

  • Which games are played;
  • Session frequency;
  • Game performance;
  • Kiosk usage;
  • Technical performance;
  • General usage patterns.

This helps us improve ARCADELX games, hardware, software and services.

6. MOTION-SENSING DATA

ARCADELX uses motion-sensing technology to allow users to interact with games using body movement.

Depending on the particular ARCADELX configuration, the system may temporarily process information relating to body position, movement or tracking points for gameplay.

Such information is used to operate the game and is not intended to identify the individual player.

We do not use this motion-sensing functionality to create facial-recognition profiles or identify individuals.

7. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payments made through ARCADELX may be processed through authorized third-party payment gateways.

Nilee Games generally receives transaction and payment-status information required to:

  • Confirm the transaction;
  • Activate the gaming session;
  • Reconcile payments;
  • Handle refunds;
  • Provide customer support.

Sensitive payment credentials such as UPI PIN, CVV and banking passwords are not collected or stored by ARCADELX.

8. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with or processed by trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate ARCADELX, including:

  • Payment gateway providers;
  • Cloud/server infrastructure providers;
  • Hosting providers;
  • Technical service providers;
  • Analytics or monitoring providers;
  • Customer-support/service providers.

Such providers may process information only for the services they provide to us and subject to applicable contractual, security and legal requirements.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, governmental authority or to protect the security and rights of Nilee Games, our users, partners or systems.

9. PARTNER / VENUE INFORMATION

ARCADELX may operate at malls, restaurants, hotels, schools, corporate offices, gyms, entertainment centres, events and other partner locations.

A location partner may have access to limited operational or transaction information necessary for operating the ARCADELX kiosk and managing its commercial relationship with Nilee Games.

Partners are not authorized to access personal information beyond what is reasonably required for their role.

Nilee Games takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through ARCADELX.

Depending on the nature of the information, security measures may include:

However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. DATA RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or processed, including:

  • Providing ARCADELX services;
  • Maintaining transaction records;
  • Customer support;
  • Accounting and financial reconciliation;
  • Security and fraud prevention;
  • Legal and regulatory requirements;
  • Resolving disputes.

Gaming and operational information may be retained in aggregated or de-identified form for longer periods for analytics and service improvement.

Where information is no longer required and there is no legal or legitimate reason to retain it, we may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it.

12. CHILDREN AND MINORS

ARCADELX may be used by children and families at certain locations.

Where ARCADELX is used by children, the relevant parent, guardian, school or venue should ensure that the child uses the kiosk appropriately and safely.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for processing a child's personal data, Nilee Games will follow the applicable requirements.

ARCADELX does not intentionally use the camera system to create or store photographs or facial profiles of children.

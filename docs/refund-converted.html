REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the cancellation and refund process applicable to users making payments for gaming sessions through ARCADELX motion-sensing gaming kiosks.

ARCADELX is a product of Nilee Games and Future Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Nilee Games", "Company", "we", "us" or "our").

1. HOW ARCADELX PAYMENT WORKS

ARCADELX allows users to:

  1. Browse the game catalogue and select an available game.
  2. Review the applicable gaming session and price.
  3. Cancel or change their game selection without making any payment.
  4. Proceed to payment when they are ready to play.
  5. Scan the designated QR code and complete the payment.
  6. Start the gaming session after successful payment confirmation.

No payment is deducted merely by selecting, viewing or cancelling a game.

A payment obligation arises only when the user proceeds with the payment and the transaction is successfully completed.

2. CANCELLATION BEFORE PAYMENT

Users are free to cancel or change their selected game at any time before completing payment.

There is:

No cancellation fee.No penalty.No refund is required because no payment has been made.

Users may return to the game-selection screen and select another game whenever the kiosk permits.

3. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION

Once a user scans the QR code and successfully completes the payment, the transaction may be confirmed by the applicable payment gateway/payment system.

After successful payment confirmation, the corresponding ARCADELX gaming session may be activated.

The payment amount, game/session details and applicable session conditions will be displayed to the user before payment wherever applicable.

4. REFUNDS AFTER SUCCESSFUL PAYMENT

Because ARCADELX is an on-demand gaming service, a successfully paid gaming session is generally non-refundable once the session has been activated, except in the circumstances described in this Policy or where a refund is required by applicable law.

Refunds may be considered where the paid service was not delivered because of a verified technical, payment or operational issue.

5. WHEN A REFUND MAY BE PROVIDED

A. Payment Successful but Game Does Not Start

If the user's payment is successfully completed but the ARCADELX gaming session is not activated because of a technical or system issue, Nilee Games may provide:

  • A full refund; or
  • A replacement gaming session,

subject to transaction verification.

B. Technical Failure During Session

If a technical problem with ARCADELX prevents the user from reasonably accessing or receiving the paid gaming service, the Company may review the transaction and provide an appropriate resolution.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Full refund;
  • Partial refund;
  • Replacement gaming session; or
  • Other appropriate resolution.

C. Duplicate Payment

If the same gaming session is accidentally paid for more than once and the duplicate transaction is verified, the additional eligible payment may be refunded.

D. Payment Deducted but Session Not Activated

If the customer's account is debited but ARCADELX does not receive or process the successful payment correctly and the gaming session is not activated, the transaction may be investigated.

If the payment is confirmed as received by Nilee Games and the service was not delivered, the transaction may be eligible for a refund or replacement session.

6. WHEN A REFUND WILL GENERALLY NOT BE PROVIDED

Once a paid gaming session has been successfully activated and delivered, a refund will generally not be provided for:

  • Change of mind after payment;
  • Changing the selected game after payment;
  • Voluntarily stopping the game;
  • Not using the entire session duration;
  • Leaving the venue during a session;
  • Failure to follow gameplay instructions;
  • Failure to follow safety instructions;
  • Personal preference regarding the game;
  • User's device, internet connection or payment application issues after successful payment and service activation.

This does not affect any rights available to consumers under applicable law.

7. PAYMENT GATEWAYS

ARCADELX may use multiple authorized payment gateways and payment service providers.

The payment process may involve:

  • UPI;
  • QR-based payments;
  • Credit cards;
  • Debit cards;
  • Net banking;
  • Wallets; and
  • Other payment methods supported by the applicable payment provider.

Nilee Games does not control the processing time of banks, payment networks or payment gateways.

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the applicable payment system and, where supported, returned to the original payment method used for the transaction.

8. FAILED OR PENDING PAYMENTS

Failed Payment

If a payment fails and the gaming session is not activated, the user should check the payment status with their bank or payment application.

If money has been debited despite the payment being shown as failed, the transaction may be automatically reversed by the applicable payment system.

Pending Payment

If a payment is shown as pending, users should avoid making repeated payments until the status is resolved, wherever reasonably possible.

Successful Payment

If the payment is successfully completed and the ARCADELX session is activated, the transaction will be treated as a completed gaming purchase subject to this Policy.

For a payment-related issue, users can contact:

To investigate a refund request, Nilee Games may verify:

  • Transaction ID;
  • Payment status;
  • Kiosk ID;
  • Session ID;
  • Game selected;
  • Session activation status;
  • Session start/end time;

  • Other transaction-related information reasonably necessary to resolve the request.

12. CHARGEBACKS AND PAYMENT DISPUTES

Customers are encouraged to contact info@nileegames.com first so that Nilee Games can investigate and attempt to resolve payment or service-related issues.

Where a customer initiates a chargeback or payment dispute through their bank, card issuer, UPI provider or payment service provider, Nilee Games may provide relevant transaction and service records to the applicable institution for dispute resolution.

13. NO CASH REFUNDS

Approved refunds for digital transactions will generally be processed electronically through the applicable payment system.

Cash refunds will not normally be provided for payments made through QR, UPI, cards, wallets or other digital payment methods.

14. FRAUDULENT REFUND CLAIMS

Nilee Games reserves the right to investigate suspected fraudulent or abusive refund requests.

Where a refund request is found to involve fraud, manipulation, false transaction information or misuse of the ARCADELX system, the Company may reject the request and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

15. PARTNER / VENUE-RELATED ISSUES

ARCADELX may be installed at malls, restaurants, hotels, schools, corporate offices, gyms, entertainment centres, events and other partner locations.

Where a gaming session is interrupted due to a venue-specific issue such as power failure, venue closure or other operational issue, Nilee Games may investigate the transaction and determine an appropriate resolution based on the circumstances and applicable commercial arrangements.

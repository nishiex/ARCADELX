Information-First: What to Expect

ARCADELX kiosks are designed to give venues a compact, accessible gaming experience. Sessions are short, clear on pricing before payment, and controlled by your movement — not by handheld controllers. The sensor processes motion data for gameplay only; it is not intended to capture photographs or function as CCTV.

Quick Sessions

Browse games, scan the QR code, complete payment and start within minutes.

Privacy-focused

Motion inputs are processed in real-time and may be stored briefly for session continuity; ARCADELX does not intentionally store player photos.

Venue Friendly

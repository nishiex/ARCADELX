Game Catalogue

Informational catalogue — each entry describes gameplay, session length, and required play space. Promotional claims are kept separate from factual descriptions.

Neon Sprint

Type: Full-body runner • Players: 1 • Session: 2–4 minutes

Move laterally to dodge obstacles and collect boosts. Recommended safe play area: 2m x 1.5m. Uses motion sensors to detect foot position and velocity.

More details

Difficulty: Moderate. Accessibility options: seated mode for gesture-based lateral moves. Not recommended for persons with severe balance issues.

Rhythm Wave

Type: Rhythm & movement • Players: 1–2 • Session: 3–5 minutes

Match beat patterns using arm and foot gestures. Recommended play area: 1.5m x 1.5m.

More details

Accessibility options: adjustable timing windows; simplified gestures for younger players.

Strike Arena

Type: Gesture combat • Players: 1–2 • Session: 4–6 minutes

Use strike and block gestures to compete in short rounds. Recommended play area: 2m x 2m.

More details

Designed for short bursts of activity; players should leave the area clear of obstacles and personal items.

Information vs. Promotion

Descriptions above focus on gameplay and safety. Promotional materials (venue advertising or partner brochures) may highlight exciting features; this catalogue keeps factual details prominent to help users make informed choices.

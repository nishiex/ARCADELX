Refund & Cancellation Policy

This page summarizes our practical refund and cancellation approach. For merchant-specific disputes or payment-provider processes, the payment provider's terms also apply.

Cancellations before payment

Users may browse and change games before payment without a cancellation fee. No charge is made until payment is completed.

Completed and activated sessions

Successfully activated gaming sessions are generally non-refundable. This is because the service is consumed in real time and sessions are consumed at the point of activation.

Refund exceptions

Refunds may be considered for verified technical, payment, or operational failures that prevent a session from being run. Duplicate verified payments may also be eligible for refund.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are normally initiated within 3–7 business days. Final crediting depends on the payment system and banking partners.

How to contact support

For refund requests or disputes, contact: info@nileegames.com. Provide your receipt or transaction reference and a brief description of the issue.

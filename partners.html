Partner With Us

Host an ARCADELX kiosk at your venue to offer visitors a short, information-first gaming experience. Below we clarify what venues typically need and how partnerships work in principle.

What hosting requires

  • Suitable, unobstructed floor space as recommended by the game(s).
  • Reliable electricity supply for the kiosk.
  • Internet connectivity may be required for payment processing, remote monitoring, and content updates. Offline payment options can be configured in some deployments.
  • Venue staff support for general supervision and basic troubleshooting.

Commercial arrangements

ARCADELX works with venue partners on commercial and operational models that suit each site. Typical partnership discussions include kiosk placement, operating hours, and mutual expectations for maintenance and customer support. Where revenue sharing is used, terms are agreed per-venue; we do not publish fixed percentages here because arrangements are customized.

To learn more or request partnership materials, click Become a Partner.

Next steps

  1. Contact our partnerships team at info@nileegames.com.
  2. We perform a short site suitability review and discuss operating models.
  3. Deploy and support: our team assists with installation and provides training material for venue staff.

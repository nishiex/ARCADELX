Safety & Responsible Use

Follow these guidelines to keep gameplay safe and enjoyable. These are practical instructions — not medical advice.

  • Maintain sufficient playing space. Clear the area of chairs, bags, and other obstacles before starting a session.
  • Follow on-screen instructions and any guidance from venue staff.
  • Keep personal items and bags outside the playing area.
  • Do not touch, move, or attempt to repair the kiosk, camera, or sensors.
  • Stop playing if you feel uncomfortable, dizzy, or unwell and seek assistance from venue staff.
  • Children and minors should be supervised by an adult as required by venue rules and local regulations.

If you have specific health concerns, consult a qualified professional. ARCADELX provides on-screen safety notices and configurable accessibility options in many games.
